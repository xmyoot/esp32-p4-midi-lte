SIM7600-H Series

SIMCom LTE Cat 4 Module

                           Product Description

                           The SIM7600-H series is the LTE Cat 4 module which
                           supports wireless communication modes of LTE-TDD/
                           LTE-FDD/HSPA+/GSM/GPRS/EDGE etc. It supports
                           maximum 150Mbps downlink rate and 50Mbps uplink rate.

Multi-        Cat 4  LCC1  The SIM7600-H series adopts LCC1 form factor and is
Band                       compatible with SIM5320/SIM5360 series(UMTS/HSPA+
                           modules), and SIM7600 series(LTE modules), which
                           enables smooth migration from 3G products to LTE
                           products, and greatly facilitates more compatible product
                           design for the customer needs.

VoLTE   Compact      FOTA  The SIM7600-H series integrates multiple satellite high
           Size       SSL  accuracy positioning GNSS systems, with multiple built-in
                           network protocols, supports drivers for main operation
TCP/IP  USB 2.0            systems (USB driver for Windows, Linux and Android etc.)
                           and software function, AT commands are compatible with
                           SIM7500/SIM7600 series modules. Meanwhile the
                           SIM7600-H series integrates main industrial standard
                           interfaces, with powerful expansibility, including abundant
                           interfaces such as UART, USB, I2C, GPIO, which is
                           suitable for main IoT applications such as telematics,
                           surveillance devices, CPE, industrial routers, and remote
                           diagnostics etc.

Key Benefits                    NOTE
                           1: The form factor of SIM7600NA-H & SIM7600G-H is LCC+LGA

u LCC1 form factor with abundant interfaces
u Suitable for LTE, UMTS and GSM networks with global coverage
u Abundant software functions: FOTA, LBS, TLS, VoLTE
u Multi-constellation GNSS receiver support

u AT commands of the SIM7600-H series are compatible with the SIM7500/SIM7600 series modules
