### Custom rules (`esp32p4_lte_cam.kicad_dru`)

- 0.09 mm clearance and track inside the P4 courtyard (0.35 mm QFN fan-out)
- RF ground gap ≥ 0.2 mm
- USB: diff gap 0.12–0.14 mm, ≤ 3 mm uncoupled, ≤ 0.15 mm skew
- MIPI: diff gap 0.12–0.14 mm, ≤ 2 mm uncoupled, ≤ 0.1 mm intra-pair skew, lanes ≤ 40 mm
- HighCurrent vias: drill ≥ 0.3 mm

## Keep-outs

- **C6 antenna:** an all-copper-layer keep-out ships inside Espressif's ESP32-C6-MINI-1 footprint. Placing the module on the left edge puts it at the board edge.
- **SIM7600 top layer:** no routing under the module except on its pads (SIMCom layout guide). Added as a rule area once the modem is placed (Phase 4).

## Library fixes applied (`scripts/fix_lcsc_pins.py`)

- EasyEDA symbols: every "unspecified" pin is given a real electrical type, so ERC means something.
- Espressif P4 symbol: VDD_ANA and VDD_BAT changed from bidirectional to power_in.
- EasyEDA footprints: locating pegs imported as zero-annular PTH become **NPTH** (switches, nano-SIM, microSD). The PIR TO-5 courtyard is rebuilt as a clean Ø9.7 mm circle.
